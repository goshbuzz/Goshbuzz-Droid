The commitments

Earn trust. Don’t ask for it.

01 /

Source you can inspect

Our work is public. Planned features are not delivered protections. Follow the documentation to see what actually exists.

02 /

Privacy without tracking

No telemetry, analytics, or identifiers by default. This page has no JavaScript, external fonts, or analytics. Hosting still involves network metadata.

03 /

Limits, stated plainly

A carrier can locate a cellular phone. The modem is closed source. A VPN moves trust; it doesn’t remove it.

No promises of perfect privacy.

Android 5–7 era devices are best-effort. Permanently locked bootloaders may never support a full install. Some banking, payment, and DRM apps will break. No software can promise perfect privacy or security.
